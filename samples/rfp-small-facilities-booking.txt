REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — MEETING ROOM BOOKING SYSTEM
RFP Reference: FB-2026-018 | Issued: June 2026 | Issuing Entity: Regional Logistics Authority

SECTION 1 — INTRODUCTION
The Authority invites proposals from qualified vendors for a cloud-based meeting room and
desk booking system to be deployed across three office locations (Doha, Dubai, Manama).
Vendors must respond to every requirement below indicating full compliance, partial
compliance, or non-compliance, with supporting evidence.

SECTION 2 — FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
2.1 The solution MUST allow employees to book meeting rooms via web and mobile app.
2.2 The solution MUST show real-time room availability synced with Outlook/Microsoft 365
    calendars.
2.3 Describe how the system handles double-booking conflicts and cancellation policies.
2.4 The solution MUST support desk booking (hot-desking) in addition to meeting rooms.
2.5 Confirm whether the system supports QR-code check-in at room entrances.

SECTION 3 — SECURITY AND DATA REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Confirm whether the vendor holds ISO 27001 or equivalent security certification.
3.2 Describe how customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
3.3 Confirm the data hosting region and whether data can be kept within the GCC.

SECTION 4 — INTEGRATION REQUIREMENTS
4.1 The solution MUST integrate with Microsoft 365 and Azure Active Directory for SSO.
4.2 Describe any available REST API for custom reporting integrations.

SECTION 5 — COMMERCIAL REQUIREMENTS
5.1 Provide your pricing model (per-user, per-room, or flat-fee) for approximately 300
    users across 3 locations.
5.2 Confirm implementation timeline and whether onsite installation support is included.
5.3 Describe your standard support hours and response times for critical issues.

END OF RFP
