KLOVERED SOLUTIONS — COMPANY CAPABILITY STATEMENT
Prepared for internal knowledge base ingestion. Last updated: June 2026.

1. SECURITY CERTIFICATIONS
Klovered Solutions holds ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification (certificate #IS-745211, valid
through March 2028) and completes an annual SOC 2 Type II audit covering security,
availability, and confidentiality trust criteria. The latest SOC 2 report (January 2026)
recorded zero exceptions. Penetration tests are performed twice yearly by CREST-accredited
third parties, with all critical and high findings remediated within 14 days. Vulnerability
scanning runs continuously across all production infrastructure using an authenticated
scanner, with results triaged weekly by the security team.

2. DATA ENCRYPTION AND KEY MANAGEMENT
All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 with keys managed in a FIPS 140-2
Level 3 validated hardware security module, and in transit using TLS 1.3 exclusively (TLS
1.0/1.1 are disabled at the load balancer). Key rotation occurs every 90 days automatically.
Customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK) are supported on the Enterprise plan, allowing
customers to revoke access unilaterally by disabling their key. Field-level encryption is
available for designated sensitive fields (e.g. national ID numbers, banking details).

3. DATA RESIDENCY AND HOSTING
The platform is hosted on Microsoft Azure with primary regions in Qatar Central (Doha) and
West Europe (Netherlands). Customers select their data residency region at contract
signature; data never leaves the selected region, including backups and disaster recovery
replicas. An on-premises deployment option is available for government clients with
sovereignty requirements, delivered as a containerized bundle deployable on customer-owned
infrastructure with quarterly patch releases.

4. SERVICE LEVELS AND SUPPORT
We commit to a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA with service credits of 10% per 0.1% shortfall,
capped at 50% of monthly fees. Support tiers: Standard (business hours, 8-hour response) and
Premium (24/7, 1-hour P1 response, dedicated technical account manager). Average P1
resolution time in 2025 was 3.2 hours. All incidents are published to a public status page
with real-time updates during active incidents.

5. IMPLEMENTATION METHODOLOGY
Implementations follow a four-phase methodology: Discovery (2 weeks), Configuration (3-4
weeks), UAT and training (2 weeks), and Hypercare (2 weeks post go-live). A typical mid-size
deployment (100-300 users) completes in 8-10 weeks; enterprise deployments (500+ users,
multiple business units) typically run 14-18 weeks. Each project is staffed with a certified
project manager, a solution architect, and a training lead. We have completed 140+
implementations with a 96% on-time delivery rate and a 4.6/5 average customer satisfaction
score on post-implementation surveys.

6. PRICING MODEL
Pricing is per-user per-month subscription with three tiers: Essential ($29/user/month),
Professional ($55/user/month), and Enterprise (custom, volume-discounted above 500 seats).
Implementation is a one-time fee typically 0.8x-1.2x first-year subscription value. No
charges for storage up to 1TB per workspace; API access is included in Professional and
above. Multi-year commitments receive 12-18% discounts; a 3-year term with annual prepayment
receives the maximum discount.

7. INTEGRATIONS AND API
The platform exposes a REST API (OpenAPI 3.1 documented) and webhooks for all core objects,
with rate limits of 1,000 requests/minute on Enterprise. Native integrations include
Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Salesforce, SAP Ariba, Oracle Fusion, and Slack. Single sign-on is
supported via SAML 2.0 and OIDC (validated against Azure AD, Okta, and Ping Identity). SCIM
2.0 handles automated user provisioning and deprovisioning, including group-based role
assignment.

8. ACCESS CONTROL AND AUDIT
Role-based access control ships with five default roles (Admin, Manager, Contributor,
Reviewer, Viewer) and unlimited custom roles with field-level permissions. All privileged
actions are captured in an immutable audit log retained for 7 years and exportable to
customer SIEM via syslog or API (Splunk and Microsoft Sentinel connectors are pre-built). IP
allowlisting, session timeout policies, and concurrent session limits are configurable per
workspace.

9. BUSINESS CONTINUITY AND DISASTER RECOVERY
The disaster recovery plan targets an RPO of 15 minutes and RTO of 4 hours, tested twice
yearly with published results shared with customers under NDA. Backups run continuously
(point-in-time recovery to any second within 35 days) plus daily snapshots retained 12
months in a geo-separate region. The business continuity plan is ISO 22301-aligned and
independently audited annually.

10. COMPANY PROFILE AND REFERENCES
Klovered Solutions was founded in 2019, is headquartered in Doha with offices in Dubai and
London, and employs 85 staff, of whom 60 are in engineering and delivery. The company is
profitable, carries no external debt, and has grown revenue 40%+ year-over-year for three
consecutive years. Reference customers include three GCC government ministries, two regional
banks, and a Fortune 500 logistics firm; contactable references are available under NDA
following shortlist confirmation.

11. COMPLIANCE AND PRIVACY
The platform complies with GDPR and Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (Law No. 13
of 2016). A Data Protection Officer is appointed and listed in our public privacy policy.
Data processing agreements incorporating EU standard contractual clauses are offered as
standard contract addenda. Data subject requests (access, erasure, portability) are
fulfilled through a self-service console within 30 days. Sub-processors are listed publicly
on our trust center and customers are notified 30 days before any sub-processor change.

12. TRAINING AND ONBOARDING
Training includes role-based live sessions (administrator, end-user, and executive tracks),
a self-paced learning portal with completion certificates, and train-the-trainer packages
for large deployments. All documentation and the platform UI are available in English and
Arabic. Post go-live, quarterly business reviews track adoption metrics; customers average
78% weekly active usage after 90 days, rising to 85%+ by month six.

13. FINANCIAL AND CONTRACTUAL TERMS
Standard payment terms are net-30 against quarterly invoicing, with annual prepayment
available at a 5% discount. Contracts are governed by the customer's local law where
required for public-sector procurement. Professional indemnity insurance of $5M and cyber
liability insurance of $10M are maintained and certificates provided upon request.

14. PRODUCT ROADMAP AND AI CAPABILITIES
The platform includes built-in AI-assisted drafting for compliance responses, automated
requirement extraction from uploaded tender documents, and a searchable answer library that
improves reuse rates over time. A public roadmap is shared quarterly with enterprise
customers under NDA; recent additions include Arabic-language AI drafting and expanded ERP
connector coverage (Oracle Fusion, Microsoft Dynamics 365).
