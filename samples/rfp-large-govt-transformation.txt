REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — DIGITAL PROCUREMENT TRANSFORMATION PROGRAMME
RFP Reference: MIN-2026-227 | Issued: June 2026 | Issuing Entity: Ministry of Public Works

SECTION 1 — INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE
The Ministry invites qualified vendors to propose an end-to-end digital transformation of
its procurement and vendor management function, covering tender publication, bid
evaluation, contract management, and vendor performance tracking across 18 directorates.
This is a multi-year programme; vendors must respond fully to every requirement in Sections
3 through 9. Partial or non-responsive submissions will be scored accordingly.

SECTION 2 — BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVES
The Ministry currently manages over 900 active contracts and issues approximately 600
tenders annually, ranging from small works orders to multi-year infrastructure contracts
exceeding $50M. The objective of this programme is to reduce average tender-to-award cycle
time by 40%, improve compliance auditability, and provide real-time visibility to
Ministry leadership across all active procurements.

SECTION 3 — SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
3.1 The vendor MUST hold a current ISO/IEC 27001 certification; provide certificate number,
    scope, and validity period.
3.2 The vendor MUST provide evidence of an independent SOC 2 Type II audit completed within
    the last 12 months, or an equivalent recognized attestation.
3.3 Describe encryption standards applied at rest and in transit, including key management
    and support for customer-managed encryption keys.
3.4 The solution MUST support SSO via SAML 2.0 or OIDC integrated with the Ministry's
    Active Directory environment.
3.5 Describe the role-based access control model, including support for custom roles,
    field-level permissions, and delegation of approval authority.
3.6 Describe your penetration testing regime, frequency, and remediation SLAs by severity.
3.7 Describe your vulnerability management program including scanning cadence and patch
    timelines for critical/high findings.
3.8 Confirm whether multi-factor authentication is enforced for all privileged accounts.
3.9 Describe your security incident response plan and customer notification timelines.
3.10 Confirm whether the platform has undergone independent code security review (SAST/DAST)
     and describe the frequency.

SECTION 4 — DATA GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENTS
4.1 All Ministry data MUST reside within national borders or, where explicitly approved,
    within the GCC. Specify all available hosting regions and residency guarantees.
4.2 Confirm compliance with applicable national data protection law and describe your data
    protection governance structure, including whether a Data Protection Officer is
    appointed.
4.3 Describe backup and disaster recovery capabilities, including RPO/RTO commitments and
    testing frequency, with evidence of the most recent DR test.
4.4 Confirm whether an on-premises or sovereign-cloud deployment option is available for
    classified or sensitive procurement categories.
4.5 Describe sub-processor disclosure practices and the notice period provided before any
    sub-processor change.
4.6 Confirm data retention, export, and deletion practices upon contract termination or
    expiry.
4.7 Describe your approach to data classification and handling of commercially sensitive
    bid information during active tender evaluation periods.

SECTION 5 — FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS — TENDER MANAGEMENT
5.1 The solution MUST support end-to-end tender lifecycle management from publication
    through award.
5.2 Describe support for structured bid submission templates and automated compliance
    checklists.
5.3 Describe how the platform supports multi-stage evaluation (technical, commercial,
    financial) with weighted scoring.
5.4 Confirm whether the solution supports sealed-bid electronic submission with
    tamper-evident timestamps.
5.5 Describe capabilities for AI-assisted extraction of requirements from uploaded tender
    documents and automated compliance matrix generation.
5.6 Describe how the platform supports collaborative evaluation across multiple evaluators
    with conflict-of-interest tracking.

SECTION 6 — FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS — CONTRACT AND VENDOR MANAGEMENT
6.1 The solution MUST provide a centralized contract repository with automated renewal and
    milestone alerts.
6.2 Describe vendor performance tracking capabilities, including scorecards and historical
    performance analytics.
6.3 Describe support for multi-currency and multi-entity contract structures.
6.4 Confirm whether the solution supports e-signature integration for contract execution.

SECTION 7 — TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
7.1 The solution MUST provide a documented REST API for integration with the Ministry's
    financial and ERP systems.
7.2 Describe available native integrations, specifically with SAP Ariba, Oracle Fusion, and
    Microsoft 365/SharePoint.
7.3 The solution MUST support automated user provisioning and deprovisioning via SCIM.
7.4 Describe the platform's uptime commitment and the service credit regime applicable to
    SLA breaches.
7.5 Confirm API rate limits and describe how the platform scales under peak concurrent load
    (e.g. tender submission deadline surges).
7.6 Describe the system architecture's approach to high availability, failover, and
    horizontal scalability.
7.7 Confirm whether the platform supports Arabic-language data entry, search, and reporting.
7.8 Describe your approach to accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA or equivalent).

SECTION 8 — IMPLEMENTATION AND SUPPORT REQUIREMENTS
8.1 Describe your implementation methodology, typical timeline for a deployment of this
    scale (18 directorates, ~1,200 users), and the project team you would assign.
8.2 Describe the training approach, including Arabic-language materials, train-the-trainer
    packages, and post-go-live adoption tracking.
8.3 Describe your support tiers, response times for priority-1 incidents, and escalation
    procedures including out-of-hours coverage.
8.4 Provide details of at least three comparable public-sector reference implementations,
    preferably within the GCC region, of similar scale.
8.5 Describe your product roadmap process and how customer feedback from government clients
    specifically is incorporated.
8.6 Confirm the availability of a dedicated technical account manager and named support
    escalation contacts for the duration of the contract.
8.7 Describe your approach to knowledge transfer and reducing dependency on vendor personnel
    over the contract term.
8.8 Describe your change management and configuration governance process for a
    multi-directorate rollout.

SECTION 9 — COMMERCIAL REQUIREMENTS
9.1 Provide your pricing model, including subscription tiers, implementation fees, and any
    volume or multi-year discounts applicable to a ~1,200-user deployment across 18
    directorates.
9.2 Confirm the company's financial standing, years in operation, and total staff dedicated
    to delivery and support.
9.3 Confirm standard payment terms and whether contract governance under national law is
    available.
9.4 Confirm professional indemnity and cyber liability insurance coverage levels, and
    whether coverage can be increased for this engagement.
9.5 Describe your approach to price stability/escalation clauses over a multi-year term.
9.6 Confirm whether performance bonds or bank guarantees can be provided as required by
    public procurement regulations.

END OF RFP
