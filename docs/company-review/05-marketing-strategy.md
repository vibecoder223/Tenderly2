# Klovered — marketing and advertising strategy

Everything here obeys BRAND.md: sentence case, short sentences, numbers carry proof, no
exclamation marks, no em dashes, and none of the banned words (seamless, unlock, empower,
leverage, revolutionize, effortless, supercharge, magic, world-class, cutting-edge).

## 1. Brand positioning

**Category:** AI RFP response software.
**Position:** the grounded one. Every answer cited to your own documents. No source, no
answer — it flags a human instead.

Positioning statement: *For bid teams at consultancies, integrators, and contractors who
lose weeks to every tender, Klovered is AI RFP response software that drafts every answer
from your own documents with a citation, unlike generic AI that guesses and legacy tools
that still make you write.*

Three pillars every asset draws from:
1. **Speed with proof.** "300 questions. 3 days, not 3 weeks."
2. **Trust by construction.** "It never guesses. It flags the gap instead."
3. **Compounding value.** "Every bid makes the next one faster."

## 2. Messaging framework

| Audience | Pain | Message | Proof point |
|---|---|---|---|
| CEO / sales director (buyer) | Skipped bids, lost deals, expert hours burned | Answer more bids with the same team | 255 requirements drafted in under 3 minutes |
| Bid manager (user) | Chasing experts, version chaos, deadline panic | One workspace from upload to submission | Review queue, approvals, template export |
| Consultant / SME (user) | Retyping the same answers every quarter | Approve drafts instead of writing from zero | Answer library, reuse counts |
| Procurement-heavy verticals | Compliance risk | Cited or flagged, never invented | 0 hallucinated answers in live test |

Objection handling, pre-written:
- "We use ChatGPT." → In a bid, answers are legally binding. ChatGPT writes confident
  answers with no facts. Klovered cites its source or flags a human. That is the difference
  between a draft and a liability.
- "We have Loopio/Responsive." → Those manage content. You still write. Klovered drafts
  every answer from your documents and shows you where each sentence came from.
- "Is our data training your model?" → No. Your documents stay in your workspace and are
  used only to answer your bids.

## 3. Brand voice

Bold, human, unfussy. Write like a sharp colleague, not a vendor. Lead with the outcome,
give the number, stop. Contractions are fine. Sentence case everywhere. If a sentence would
survive on a billboard, it's right; if it needs a comma splice of adjectives, cut it.

40% teardown/education, 30% build-in-public, 20% proof, 10% opinion. Every post ends with
one question or one soft CTA, never both.

## 5. Paid ads (only after 10 reference customers)

### LinkedIn ads

- **Audience:** job titles bid manager, proposal manager, presales lead, sales director,
  managing director; industries IT services, government administration contractors,
  engineering services; company size 11–500; regions Gulf (QA, AE, SA), UK, then EU.
- **Formats:** single-image + document ad (carousel of the live-test numbers).
- **Headline options:**
  - "300 questions. 3 days, not 3 weeks."
  - "Stop retyping. Start winning."
  - "Every requirement, answered and cited."
- **Body copy (single image):** "Klovered reads the RFP, drafts every answer from your own
  documents, and flags what it can't support. Your team reviews and ships. Book a demo on
  your own RFP."
- **Creative direction:** brand-exact. Warm-white canvas, one signal-green moment, Clash
  Display headline, a real product frame (cited chip + no-source flag visible). No robots,
  no glowing brains, no stock handshakes.
- **CTA:** "Book a demo" → demo page with the 3-minute live-run video.

### Google ads

- **Keywords (exact/phrase, high intent only):** rfp response software, ai rfp software,
  rfp automation tool, proposal automation software, bid writing software, respond to rfp
  faster, loopio alternative, responsive rfpio alternative, security questionnaire
  automation.
- **Negative keywords:** free, template, example, sample, jobs, course, meaning.
- **Ad copy:** headline "AI RFP response software" / "Cited answers from your own docs" /
  "3 days, not 3 weeks"; description "Upload the RFP. Klovered drafts every answer with a
  citation and flags the gaps. Book a demo on your own tender."
- **Landing pages:** brand landing page for category terms; dedicated comparison pages for
  "alternative" terms (propello vs loopio, vs responsive); the FAQ + JSON-LD structure
  already on the landing page feeds AI-overview citations too.
- **Conversion strategy:** one conversion event only (demo booked). No newsletter goal, no
  whitepaper gate. Budget $1.5–3k/mo initially, kill any keyword without a demo in 60 clicks.

## 6. Content and SEO

- Publish every teardown post (above) as a blog article two weeks after the LinkedIn version.
- Comparison pages (vs Loopio, vs Responsive, vs ChatGPT-for-RFPs) are the highest-intent
  SEO assets in this category; write them from the capability matrix in the deck.
- Every design partner yields one case study with numbers, on a template: bids answered,
  hours saved, reuse rate, one quote from the bid manager.
- AI-SEO: the landing page's definition block, FAQ schema, and HowTo schema are already
  built for being quoted by AI assistants; keep them current as features ship.

## 7. What we do not do (yet)

No paid ads before reference customers. No events/booths in year 1. No generic AI thought
leadership. No Twitter/X (buyers aren't there). No gated PDFs. Focus wins.
