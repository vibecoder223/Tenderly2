# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Bid managers, proposal writers, and SMEs at B2B companies answering RFPs under deadline pressure. They live in this tool for hours at a stretch during a bid: triaging extracted requirements, drafting and reviewing answers, chasing approvals. Context: office hours, laptop or large monitor, light ambient environments, dense reading workloads (hundreds of questions per deal).

## Product Purpose

Klovered ingests RFP documents, extracts every requirement with AI, drafts grounded answers from the company's knowledge base, and routes them through SME review to a final export. Success = a team answers a 300-question RFP in days instead of weeks, and trusts every citation. The UI's job is throughput and trust: fast scanning, clear status, zero ambiguity about what needs a human next.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, quietly technical. Linear/Cursor discipline transplanted to a light canvas: typography does the talking, monospace details signal rigor, color is spent only on state and brand moments. The tool should feel like professional equipment, not office software.

## Anti-references

- Generic B2B admin templates (Bootstrap-admin energy, identical card grids, hero-metric tiles).
- SaaS-cream marketing softness: oversized rounded cards, pastel washes, decorative illustration.
- Dark-mode-by-reflex dev-tool cosplay. The precision comes from type and density, not a black background.
- Current weakness to fix: typography reads default/timid; nothing about the type says "this product is sharp."

## Design Principles

1. **Type is the interface.** Hierarchy, scale contrast, and mono-vs-sans texture carry the design. If a screen looks good with borders removed, the typography is right.
2. **Density is respect.** Users process hundreds of questions; give them scannable rows, tabular numerals, and tight rhythm, not padded cards.
3. **Color = state.** Forest green is brand and action; status hues mean exactly one thing each; everything else stays ink-on-paper.
4. **Show the machine's work.** Confidence scores, citations, token counts, pipeline stages: expose them in mono detail rather than hiding them behind friendly abstraction.
5. **Calm speed.** Motion is sub-200ms, exponential ease-out, and only where it explains a state change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, 3:1 large text/UI, full keyboard navigation, visible focus rings, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored. Status never communicated by color alone (always paired with a label or icon).
